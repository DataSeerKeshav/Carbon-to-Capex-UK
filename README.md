# Carbon-to-Capex UK

**Question:** Which decarbonisation levers should UK cement plants fund first, under UK ETS carbon prices and the UK CBAM from January 2027, and how does the answer change when costs and prices move?

## What this project does
1. Builds a site-level table of UK integrated cement plants, comparing satellite-based emissions estimates with audited UK ETS emissions.
2. Estimates the cost, NPV and cost per tonne of CO₂ abated for 8 decarbonisation levers under three carbon price scenarios.
3. Chooses the best portfolio of levers for a given capex budget with an optimisation model, and tests it across 500 simulated scenarios.
4. Extracts targets and project claims from company reports with an AI pipeline, with every claim checked against its source page.
